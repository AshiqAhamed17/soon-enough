# Soon Enough

> "Collecting places. Chasing memories. Becoming who I'm meant to be."
> 

---

# The Vision

This project is deeply personal.

Do not think of it as a portfolio.

Do not think of it as a travel website.

Do not think of it as a restaurant tracker.

Instead, think of it as my digital sanctuary.

A place I return to after difficult weeks.

A place that reminds me how beautiful life can be.

A place that captures the moments that slowly shape who I become.

Every café.
Every city.
Every restaurant.
Every street.
Every sunset.

They aren't locations.

They're chapters.

This website should make me smile every single time I open it.

Years from now, I want to revisit this website and immediately remember how those moments felt.

The goal is emotional, not informational.

---

# What This Website Should Say About Me

Without reading a biography, visitors should understand who I am.

Someone who:

- values experiences over possessions
- loves beautiful cafés
- enjoys thoughtful design
- loves travelling
- enjoys slowing down
- appreciates architecture
- enjoys meaningful conversations
- believes hard work creates freedom
- dreams big but enjoys the little moments
- wants to build an extraordinary life

The website should quietly communicate ambition, curiosity, optimism and gratitude.

Never arrogance.

Never luxury for the sake of luxury.

Instead:

Purpose.

Taste.

Curiosity.

Wonder.

---

# Design Philosophy

Imagine if Apple designed a personal journal.

Imagine if Airbnb designed a travel diary.

Imagine if Monocle Magazine became an interactive website.

Imagine if Notion had emotion.

Imagine if Arc Browser became a scrapbook.

That is the feeling.

The website should feel timeless.

Minimal.

Elegant.

Premium.

Intentional.

Every pixel should feel like it belongs.

Nothing should exist simply because it looks cool.

Everything should have a purpose.

Whitespace is part of the design.

Silence is part of the design.

The experience should feel calm.

# Technology

Use:

- Next.js 15 (App Router)
- TypeScript
- Tailwind CSS
- Framer Motion
- Lucide Icons
- shadcn/ui where appropriate

No backend.

No authentication.

No database.

No CMS.

Everything should be static.

Everything should be editable from local TypeScript files.

Deployable directly to Vercel.

---

# Design Rules

No loud gradients.

No glassmorphism everywhere.

No futuristic cyberpunk aesthetics.

No gaming UI.

No unnecessary animations.

Avoid visual noise.

Instead:

Large photography.

Elegant typography.

Beautiful spacing.

Warm neutral colours.

Subtle motion.

Rounded corners.

Magazine-inspired layouts.

---
